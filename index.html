About Us

At Purple Design, we believe memories are more than moments they're pieces of the heart, stitched in color, light, and love. Born from the joy of preserving little things a smile, a date, a shared story we turn your emotions into tangible keepsakes: custom story cards, birthday calendar cards, and designs that speak without words. Each piece is handmade with care, blending design with emotion, so you can carry your loved ones not just in memory, but in your pocket, your wall, your world. Because every heart has a story, and we’re here to help you hold onto it beautifully 💜
